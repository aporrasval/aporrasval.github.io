Dr. Antonio J. Porras-Valverde — Personal Academic Website
https://aporrasval.github.io

Personal website for Dr. Antonio J. Porras-Valverde, an astrophysicist
specializing in supermassive black hole formation, galaxy evolution, and
semi-analytic modeling of cosmic structure. The site serves as a hub for
research, outreach, and professional information.

---

Sections
--------

About Me
  Background, photo collage, and a brief personal and academic biography.

Research
  Overview of current and past research projects, including work on black
  hole spin evolution, galaxy quenching, and stellar-to-halo mass relations.
  Features plots from simulations (IllustrisTNG, MAD SAGE).

MAD SAGE
  Dedicated section explaining the MAD SAGE semi-analytic model — what it is,
  how semianalytic models work, and why black hole spin matters for galaxy
  feedback.

Cenca Bridge
  Information about Cenca Bridge Inc., a nonprofit co-led by Dr. Porras-Valverde
  to build bridges between Latin American scientists and the international
  research community. Includes the 2024 IAU Development Prize recognition.

Media / Press
  Coverage and interviews from La Nación, Yale News, Vanderbilt, Futurum,
  and the Costa Rican National Academy of Sciences.

CV
  Downloadable PDF of the full curriculum vitae (January 2026 version).
